## Paper

This repository accompanies the paper:

**Applications of Symmetry-Enhanced Physics-Informed Neural Networks in High-Pressure Gas Flow Simulations in Pipelines**

[Read the paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-8994/16/5/538)

The paper presents applications of symmetry-enhanced Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) to:
- the 1D inviscid Burgers equation;
- the 1D viscous Burgers equation;
- the 1D Euler equations for gas dynamics.
